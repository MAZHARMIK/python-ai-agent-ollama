Hiring Process - Interview Stages
Candidates go through 5 stages: resume screening, technical round 1, technical round 2, managerial round and HR round.

Hiring Process - Offer
The offer letter is sent within 5 working days after the final round. A candidate has 7 days to accept the offer.

Hiring Process - Referral Bonus
Employees who refer a candidate that joins get a referral bonus of 25,000 rupees, paid after the new joiner completes 90 days.

Hiring Process - Joining
New joiners receive a laptop within one day of joining. HR sends the onboarding checklist two days before the joining date.
