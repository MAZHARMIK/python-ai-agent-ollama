HR - Working Hours
Core working hours at Zorvex are 10 AM to 4 PM. Employees must complete 8 hours a day, and the office is open Monday to Friday.

HR - Work From Home
Employees can work from home up to 2 days a week with manager approval. Fully remote work needs approval from the HR director.

HR - Onboarding
On the first day, new joiners complete HR formalities, collect their ID card and attend an orientation session. A buddy is assigned for the first 30 days.

HR - Performance Reviews
Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Ratings are on a scale of 1 to 5, and a rating of 4 or above makes an employee eligible for a bonus.

HR - Notice Period
The standard notice period is 60 days. Employees in the first 6 months of service have a notice period of 30 days.
