Travel Expense - Reimbursement
Submit expense claims within 30 days of the trip with all receipts attached. Approved claims are reimbursed within 7 working days.

Travel Expense - Flights
Economy class is allowed for flights under 6 hours. Business class needs approval from a director for longer flights.

Travel Expense - Daily Allowance
The daily allowance is 2,000 rupees for domestic trips and 60 US dollars for international trips. Hotel bills are reimbursed up to 6,000 rupees per night.

Travel Expense - Receipts
Claims without receipts are rejected, except for expenses under 500 rupees, which can be self-declared.
