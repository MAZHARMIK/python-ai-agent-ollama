Leave Policy - Casual Leave
Every employee at Zorvex Technologies gets 12 days of casual leave per year. Casual leave must be applied at least one day in advance and cannot be carried forward.

Leave Policy - Earned Leave
Employees earn 1.5 days of earned leave per month, which is 18 days per year. Up to 30 days of earned leave can be carried forward to the next year.

Leave Policy - Sick Leave
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days.

Leave Policy - Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is 10 working days and must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

Leave Policy - Applying for Leave
Leave must be applied for in the Zorvex PeopleHub portal and approved by the reporting manager. An unapproved absence of more than 3 days is treated as unauthorized absence.
