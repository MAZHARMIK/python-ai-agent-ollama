Medical Policy - Health Insurance
Every employee and their family is covered under a family floater health insurance of 5 lakh rupees. Parents can be added at an extra cost.

Medical Policy - Claims
Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals. For other hospitals, reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of discharge.

Medical Policy - Long Medical Leave
If an employee needs more than 10 days of sick leave, they can apply for long medical leave of up to 60 days. A doctor's certificate and HR approval are required.

Medical Policy - Maternity Cover
Hospitalization for childbirth is covered up to 75,000 rupees. The maternity cover starts after 9 months of service.

Medical Policy - Health Checkup
Every employee gets one free annual health checkup at a partner clinic. Dental and eye treatment are not covered by the insurance.
